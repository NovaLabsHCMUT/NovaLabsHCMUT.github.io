Nhung san pham ky thuat thuc te do NovaLabs nghien cuu va che tao

AGV - Autonomous Guided Vehicle

Robot van chuyen tu hanh, dieu huong bang cam bien LIDAR va thuat toan SLAM de lap ban do va tu dinh vi trong moi truong kho bai, nha may.

Drone

May bay khong nguoi lai duoc thiet ke va lap trinh noi bo, tich hop he thong dieu khien bay va truyen du lieu thoi gian thuc.

Hexapod Robot

Robot 6 chan mo phong chuyen dong con trung, ung dung thuat toan dang di va dieu khien dong hoc nghich de di chuyen tren dia hinh phuc tap.

Robot Arm

Canh tay robot nhieu bac tu do, lap trinh dieu khien theo quy dao, phu hop cho cac ung dung lap rap va pick-and-place.

Robot Pneumatic

He thong robot khi nen dieu khien co cau chuyen dong qua xi-lanh va van dien tu, ung dung trong day chuyen san xuat tu dong.

Robot Twin

He thong digital twin mo phong va giam sat robot trong moi truong ao theo thoi gian thuc, ho tro kiem thu thuat toan truoc khi trien khai thuc te.
